FAQ

Common questions

What clients usually ask before we start.

How long does a website take?

A landing page takes about 7–10 days, a standard business website about 2–4 weeks, and a larger multi-service or e-commerce site about 5–8 weeks. The clock starts once I have your content, logo and photos.

How does payment work?

Three installments: 50% before work starts, 30% when the site is finished and you approve it, and the final 20% when everything is handed over.

Do I own my domain and website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and at handover you get the admin login, an illustrated user guide and full control of the site.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes. Business websites come with an admin panel so you can change text, photos and prices without a developer. I include step-by-step instructions at handover.

Can you guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee a ranking. What I do is build a technically clean site, write content around what your customers actually search for and submit the sitemap to Google on launch day. You can see results from past projects in the Results section.

What is AI search optimization (AIO)?

SEO helps your site show up in Google results. AI search optimization helps ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews understand your business so they mention it when people ask. I do both together: clear answers to real questions, FAQs, schema and llms.txt.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. I work with clients in the U.S. and Vietnam over Zalo, Telegram, email or Google Meet, and share a preview link for your feedback before launch.
