What I do

One person, design to search

No hand-offs between a designer, a developer and an SEO agency. The plan stays in one head.

Strategy & structure

Customer and competitor research, then a sitemap where every page drives one action: a call, a booking, an application.

Web design

Custom design for desktop and phone, copy, photography direction and a design system that keeps every page consistent.

Build & editors

Lightweight PHP and JavaScript builds or WordPress, with simple editors so clients update text, photos, jobs and prices themselves.

SEO

Technical SEO, titles and descriptions, schema markup, sitemaps, Google Business Profile and Search Console.

AI search (AIO)

Answer-first content, FAQs and structured data that help ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews understand and cite a business.

Launch & handover

Hosting, domains, SSL, form email delivery, real-device testing and an illustrated guide for the client.
