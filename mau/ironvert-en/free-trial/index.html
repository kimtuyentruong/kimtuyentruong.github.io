FAQ

About your free trial

If you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, joint problems or are pregnant, check with your doctor before your session and let your coach know.
Can I reschedule my trial?
Yes. Call us or reply to your confirmation text with your code to move it to another day.
Can I bring a friend?
Absolutely. Each person books their own trial so the coach can prepare enough space and equipment.
Do I need to know how to swim for a swim trial?
No. The swim trial is designed for adults who can’t swim yet, in the shallow lane with a coach.
