Most people don’t quit because they’re lazy. They get bored, don’t know what to do, or their work schedule never lines up with class times. IRONVERT was built to remove each of those reasons.

Quick answers

Questions before your free trial class

Questions about pricing, freezes or transfers? The Membership page has the full details.

If you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, joint problems or are pregnant, check with your doctor before training and let your coach know at your first session.
Is the trial class really free?
In this demo, your first trial is free: one group class of your choice plus a 20-minute body composition scan. After that, drop-in classes are $20 each (sample price).
I’ve never trained before. Can I keep up in a combat class?
Yes. Kickboxing and Boxing have Level 1 classes for beginners that focus on technique and reflexes first. We suggest waiting 4–6 weeks before Muay Thai or HIIT.
What should I bring?
Workout clothes, training shoes (combat and yoga classes are barefoot) and a water bottle. Gloves and hand wraps are provided for your trial. Elite includes towel service.
Is there parking?
All three clubs have bike racks. Riverside has a free on-site lot; Downtown offers validated parking in the garage next door.
