Why members love it

One hour, and your head feels clear

Kickboxing works almost your whole body: planted legs, rotating hips, shoulders driving every strike. When you’re remembering combos and keeping pace with the pads, there’s no room left in your head for work email.

We won’t promise how much weight you’ll lose or how fast. What we can say: train 2–3 times a week and you’ll notice a real difference in your stamina and reflexes.

FAQ

Before you glove up

If you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, a shoulder or knee injury, or are pregnant, check with your doctor before starting kickboxing.
Will I have to spar with other people?
Levels 1 and 2 train only on pads and heavy bags. Light sparring happens only at Level 3, in full protective gear with a coach right there, and you can always opt out.
Will kickboxing make me bulky?
Kickboxing builds endurance and reflexes far more than muscle mass. Riverside also runs a women-only class on Thursday evenings.
How long until Level 2?
Usually 8–12 weeks at two classes a week. Everyone is different; your coach will tell you when you’re ready.
Does it cost extra on top of my membership?
No. Kickboxing is included in Core and Elite. Base members can buy $20 drop-in classes (sample price).
