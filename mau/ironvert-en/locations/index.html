FAQ

Before you visit

Are there showers and hair dryers?
Yes. Every club has separate men’s and women’s locker rooms with showers, shampoo, body wash and hair dryers.
Can kids use the club?
Under-16s can use the pool and swim lessons with an adult present. The weight floor and combat classes are for ages 16 and up.
What are your holiday hours?
Holidays are 6 AM–8 PM. We close on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day; holiday hours are posted in the app two weeks ahead (sample).
