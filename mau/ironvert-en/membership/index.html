Membership policies

Clear up front, no surprises later

Sample policies to show the layout. A real club should have them reviewed by its own legal counsel.

Membership freeze

Memberships of 6 months or longer can be frozen for up to 60 days a year for illness, travel or pregnancy, with supporting documents. Give at least 1 day’s notice.

Transfers

You can transfer your membership to someone else once, for a $25 fee (sample). The new member completes an orientation before training.

Refunds

Cancel within the first 7 days having used no more than 2 visits: full refund minus visits used at the drop-in rate. After 7 days, a freeze or transfer applies.

Access hours

Every membership works during all opening hours. There are no off-peak-only plans in this demo.

Health

Members complete a health questionnaire at sign-up. Anyone with a medical condition should get their doctor’s approval before high-intensity training.

Your data

Sign-up details are used only to manage your membership and contact you about bookings. You can ask to view, correct or delete them at any time.

FAQ

Membership questions

Not sure which plan fits? Try a class first and your coach will suggest one based on your schedule and goals.

Can I pay monthly?
Yes. Every plan can be billed monthly by card with no long-term contract. Choosing a 3-, 6- or 12-month term paid up front unlocks the term discount (sample policy).
How do I upgrade from Base to Core mid-term?
You pay the prorated difference for the days left on your membership. No need to buy a new one.
Can Elite members bring a guest?
Yes, one guest visit per month, registered in advance at the front desk. Guests complete the same health questionnaire as members.
Do Elite PT sessions roll over?
PT sessions are used within the month and don’t roll over. You can swap them for 2 small-group Reformer classes instead.
Do you offer student discounts?
In this demo, students with a valid ID get 10% off Base and Core. It can’t be combined with the group sign-up discount.
