FAQ

Choosing the right class

Intensity ratings are a general guide. If you have a medical condition or an old injury, check with your doctor before choosing a high-intensity class.
How many disciplines should I train each week?
Beginners do best with two in the first month: one strength or combat class and one mobility class like Yoga or Pilates. Add more as you get stronger.
What does “High” intensity mean?
Your heart rate stays up for most of the class, with short rests between rounds. Coaches still offer an easier option for every move.
Do Reformer classes cost extra?
Mat Pilates is included in Core. Small-group Reformer uses a separate class pack; Elite already includes 4 per month (sample prices on the Membership page).
