Cooling the City from Above

AOn a summer afternoon, the centre of a large city can be several degrees warmer than the countryside around it. Researchers call this the urban heat island effect. Dark roofs, roads and car parks absorb sunlight during the day and release it slowly at night, so streets that should cool down after sunset often stay warm until morning.

BOne response has been to cover roofs with plants. A green roof usually consists of a waterproof layer, a thin growing medium and hardy species such as sedum, which need little water. Unlike a dark surface, the plants use part of the sun’s energy to evaporate water, a process that cools the air immediately above them. In one study of office buildings, rooms directly under a planted roof needed noticeably less air conditioning during heatwaves.

CGreen roofs are not without problems. They add weight, so older buildings may need to be strengthened first, and the initial cost is higher than that of a conventional roof. Some owners also underestimate maintenance: although sedum is tough, weeds can spread quickly if nobody inspects the roof for a whole season.

DFor this reason, many cities now promote a simpler option: painting roofs white or pale grey. A reflective coating sends much of the sunlight back into the sky and can be applied in a single day. Its cooling effect on the building is often similar to that of a green roof, but it does little for wildlife and cannot store rainwater.

EPlanners increasingly argue that the two approaches should be combined rather than compared. Reflective roofs can be used where buildings cannot carry extra weight, while planted roofs are placed where they also reduce flooding and provide space for insects. What matters most, they say, is that decisions are made street by street, using local temperature maps rather than a single rule for the whole city.

2Sedum needs to be watered every day during the summer.

3Most green roofs are built on new buildings rather than old ones.

4Reflective roofs provide a good habitat for wildlife.

§ 02 Sau khi làm bài

Ba thói quen giúp Reading lên nhanh hơn

i.

Đọc câu hỏi trước, gạch từ khoá

Với True/False/Not Given, gạch chủ ngữ và động từ chính. Not Given thường xuất hiện khi bài đọc không nói gì về một chi tiết cụ thể.

ii.

Chú ý từ đồng nghĩa

Câu hỏi hiếm khi lặp lại đúng từ trong bài. “Not checking regularly” ứng với “nobody inspects the roof for a whole season”.

iii.

Ghi sổ lỗi sau mỗi đề

Chép lại câu sai, lý do sai và đoạn chứa đáp án. Sau 5 đề bạn sẽ thấy dạng câu mình hay mất điểm.
